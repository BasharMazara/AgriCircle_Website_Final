Frequently Asked Questions

How can I join AgriCircle?

Simply fill out the contact form above with your details and mention your interest in joining our community. We'll get back to you with membership information.

Is AgriCircle free to join?

Yes! AgriCircle is a free community platform designed to support agricultural professionals and students worldwide.

Can I contribute to projects?

Absolutely! We encourage all members to participate in collaborative projects and share their expertise with the community.

Do you offer training programs?

Yes, we regularly organize workshops, webinars, and training sessions. Check our services page for more information.

Visit Our Campus

Our headquarters is located at the beautiful Jericho Agricultural Campus in Palestine. We welcome visitors and encourage face-to-face collaboration whenever possible.

Campus Facilities:

  • Modern research laboratories
  • Demonstration farms and greenhouses
  • Conference and meeting rooms
  • Library and resource center
  • Student collaboration spaces

Our Impact Projects

💧 Smart Irrigation Training Program

A comprehensive training initiative that empowered over 200 farmers across multiple regions with water-efficient irrigation techniques. This project resulted in an average 40% reduction in water usage while maintaining crop yields.

Key Achievements:
  • Trained 200+ farmers in smart irrigation
  • 40% reduction in water usage
  • Maintained 100% crop yield efficiency
  • Implemented in 15 different regions

Duration: 6 months | Budget: $50,000 | Status: Completed

🚀 Youth Agri-Tech Hackathon

An innovative hackathon that connected 50+ students and young professionals to design AI-based crop monitoring tools. The event fostered creativity and resulted in three viable startup ideas.

Key Achievements:
  • 50+ participants from 12 universities
  • 15 innovative project submissions
  • 3 startup companies launched
  • $25,000 in seed funding awarded

Duration: 3 days | Budget: $15,000 | Status: Annual Event

🌱 Sustainable Soil Management Program

A community-focused initiative that partnered with local farming communities to implement regenerative agriculture practices, improving soil health and long-term sustainability.

Key Achievements:
  • Improved soil health in 500+ acres
  • 25% increase in organic matter
  • Reduced chemical fertilizer use by 60%
  • Trained 150 community leaders

Duration: 12 months | Budget: $75,000 | Status: Ongoing

📱 Mobile Farm Management App

Development of a comprehensive mobile application that helps farmers track crop health, weather patterns, and market prices. The app has been downloaded by over 10,000 users worldwide.

Key Features:
  • Real-time weather monitoring
  • Crop health tracking with AI
  • Market price alerts
  • Community forum integration

Duration: 8 months | Budget: $40,000 | Status: Live & Maintained

Return Policy

Understanding our refund and return policies for AgriCircle services, training programs, and memberships.

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Our Commitment to Satisfaction

At AgriCircle, we are committed to providing high-quality services and educational programs to our agricultural community. We understand that sometimes circumstances change, and we strive to be fair and reasonable in our return and refund policies.

This Return Policy applies to all paid services, training programs, consultancy services, and premium memberships offered by AgriCircle. Please read this policy carefully before making any purchases.

Service Categories and Return Policies

Training Programs and Workshops

Online Training Courses
  • Full Refund: Available within 7 days of purchase if less than 20% of content has been accessed
  • Partial Refund: 50% refund available within 14 days if less than 50% of content has been completed
  • No Refund: After 14 days or if more than 50% of content has been accessed
In-Person Workshops
  • Full Refund: Available up to 14 days before the workshop date
  • 50% Refund: Available 7-14 days before the workshop date
  • 25% Refund: Available 3-7 days before the workshop date
  • No Refund: Within 3 days of the workshop or after attendance

Consultancy Services

  • Initial Consultation: Full refund if not satisfied within 24 hours of the first session
  • Ongoing Projects: Refund available for unused hours with 7 days notice
  • Completed Work: No refund for delivered consultancy reports or completed project phases

Premium Memberships

  • Monthly Subscriptions: Cancel anytime, no refund for current month
  • Annual Subscriptions: Pro-rated refund available within first 30 days
  • Lifetime Memberships: 30-day money-back guarantee

Digital Products and Resources

  • E-books and Guides: 7-day refund period from purchase date
  • Software Tools: 14-day trial period, refund available if not satisfied
  • Mobile Apps: Follow respective app store refund policies

Refund Process

How to Request a Refund

  1. Contact Us: Email refunds@agricircle.com with your request
  2. Provide Information: Include your order number, purchase date, and reason for refund
  3. Review Process: We will review your request within 2-3 business days
  4. Refund Processing: Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days

Required Information for Refund Requests

  • Full name and email address used for purchase
  • Order number or transaction ID
  • Date of purchase
  • Specific service or product name
  • Reason for refund request
  • Any relevant supporting documentation

Special Circumstances

Medical or Emergency Situations

We understand that unexpected medical emergencies or personal crises can prevent participation in our programs. In such cases, we may offer:

  • Full refund with appropriate documentation
  • Credit toward future programs
  • Transfer to another participant (with approval)

Technical Issues

If you experience technical difficulties that prevent access to our services:

  • We will first attempt to resolve the technical issue
  • If unresolvable, a full refund will be provided
  • Alternative access methods may be offered when possible

Program Cancellations

If AgriCircle cancels a program or service:

  • Full refund will be automatically processed
  • Alternative dates or programs may be offered
  • Credit toward future services will be available

Non-Refundable Items

The following items are generally non-refundable:

  • Completed consultancy work and delivered reports
  • Physical materials that have been shipped
  • Services that have been fully consumed or utilized
  • Custom development work that has been completed
  • Third-party services purchased on your behalf
  • Administrative fees and processing charges

Exceptions

Exceptions to non-refundable items may be made in cases of:

  • Significant service quality issues
  • Misrepresentation of service capabilities
  • Technical failures on our part
  • Legal or regulatory requirements

Refund Methods

Payment Method

Refunds will be processed using the same payment method used for the original purchase:

  • Credit Cards: 5-7 business days to appear on statement
  • PayPal: 3-5 business days
  • Bank Transfers: 7-10 business days
  • Other Methods: As per the payment provider's policy

Processing Fees

Please note that payment processing fees charged by third-party providers are generally non-refundable and may be deducted from refund amounts.

Dispute Resolution

Internal Resolution

If you are not satisfied with our refund decision, you may:

  • Request a review by our customer service manager
  • Provide additional documentation or information
  • Escalate to our executive team for final review

External Resolution

For unresolved disputes, you may seek resolution through:

  • Consumer protection agencies in your jurisdiction
  • Credit card chargeback procedures (if applicable)
  • Alternative dispute resolution services

Policy Updates

This Return Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. We will notify users of significant changes through our website or email. The updated policy will apply to purchases made after the effective date of the changes.

For purchases made before policy changes, the original policy terms at the time of purchase will continue to apply.

Fair and Transparent

Our return policy reflects our commitment to fairness and transparency in all our business dealings. We believe that clear policies help build trust within our agricultural community and ensure that everyone understands their rights and responsibilities.
